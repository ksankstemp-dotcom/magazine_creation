✍️ 2. On-Screen Typography & Natural-Language Smart Font Search

Directly edit and style any text element on the magazine proof:

🖱️ Direct Click-to-Inspect

Click any title, subhead, kicker, body paragraph, or page number on screen to bring up the floating Typography & Color Inspector.

🔍 Natural-Language Font Search

Type keywords to discover matched fonts: "Balloon Animal" (Bubble/Fun), "Funky" (Retro), "San Serif Script", "Elegant and Bold", "Signature Calligraphy", or "Clean Architectural".

🎨 Title Dual-Tone Color Balance

Choose from 8 curated color harmonies (Gold + White, Champagne Bronze, Sapphire Platinum, Sunset Coral) or customize individual primary and secondary word tones.

🔠 Letter Spacing & Sizing

Adjust font size, line-height, letter spacing (kerning), and text casing (UPPERCASE, Title Case, lowercase) in real time.

⚡
Try It: You don't need to hunt through thousands of fonts. Simply type what mood you want (e.g. "Balloon animal" or "Funky") into the font search box and select from the curated suggestions!
